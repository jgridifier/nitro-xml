# Real PowerPoint-authored .pptx corpus (#96)

Every file here was written by Microsoft PowerPoint (per `docProps/app.xml`, recorded as `authoring_app`) and is redistributable under the licence in its row. Machine-readable fields for `fixtures/manifest.json` (#4) are in `sources.json` next to this file.

Selection: candidates from the sources below, filtered to Microsoft PowerPoint authoring, no e-mail address in `docProps/core.xml`, at most 2 MB, deduplicated by sha256, then chosen greedily for feature coverage (features are detected from the package parts; see the `features` field). Files are committed byte-for-byte as published (not re-saved), so they keep what Office wrote.

Excluded on purpose: documents that were scraped from third-party websites (e.g. POI's `integration/stress*` set and files named after web URLs), Microsoft-authored templates, and anything whose licence is not one of public domain, CC0, CC-BY-4.0, MIT, Apache-2.0 or BSD.

## Licence notes

- **Apache POI (`poi-*`)**: from `test-data/` of apache/poi at the commit in each URL. The repository is distributed by the ASF under Apache-2.0 (`legal/LICENSE`, no exceptions listed for test-data), and these files ship in ASF source releases. Many were attached to ASF Bugzilla reports as minimal reproductions and committed by POI committers; Apache-2.0 §5 covers contributions submitted to the project.
- **python-docx / python-pptx (`python-docx-*`, `python-pptx-*`)**: test files of those MIT-licensed projects, mostly authored by the maintainer (Steve Canny).
- **U.S. federal government (`usgov-*`)**: works of the U.S. federal government are not subject to copyright in the U.S. (17 U.S.C. §105); the agency's reuse policy is linked as evidence. Recorded as `LicenseRef-US-Government-Work`.
- Author names in `docProps/core.xml` were left as published (they are part of what Office wrote and are already public in the source repositories); no file contains an e-mail address in its core properties.
