# Real Word-authored .docx corpus (#95)

Every file here was written by Microsoft Word (per `docProps/app.xml`, recorded as `authoring_app`) and is redistributable under the licence in its row. Machine-readable fields for `fixtures/manifest.json` (#4) are in `sources.json` next to this file.

Selection: candidates from the sources below, filtered to Microsoft Word authoring, no e-mail address in `docProps/core.xml`, at most 2 MB, deduplicated by sha256, then chosen greedily for feature coverage (features are detected from the package parts; see the `features` field). Files are committed byte-for-byte as published (not re-saved), so they keep what Office wrote.

Excluded on purpose: documents that were scraped from third-party websites (e.g. POI's `integration/stress*` set and files named after web URLs), Microsoft-authored templates, and anything whose licence is not one of public domain, CC0, CC-BY-4.0, MIT, Apache-2.0 or BSD.
