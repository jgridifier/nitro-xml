Open Office files in the browser

A Rust and WebAssembly viewer for Word, Excel and PowerPoint files. Every demo opens a sample straight away, or drop in your own: the file is processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded.

Full is the complete viewer frame: title bar, ribbon, panes and status bar. Embedded is the single-row toolbar a host app gets from <nitro-docx mode="embedded"> (design note). You can switch between them on any demo page.
